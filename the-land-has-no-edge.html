THE LAND HAS NO EDGE

TAPE 14 · RECOVERED · DO NOT DUPLICATE

Follow the SIGNAL to the televisions. Read all 7 bulletins.

Something thin follows you. Later, something tall listens for you.

WASD move · mouse look · SHIFT run · SPACE jump · E dash · C sneak / slide · Q camera · F G R items · H help
Warning: flashing imagery, sudden loud sound, jumpscares.

PAUSE

GOAL The arrow at the top of the screen always points to your next objective. Chapter 1: read 7 televisions, then collect 3 keys, then reach the exit door. Chapter 2: start the generators in 4 bunkers, then reach the mast.

THE THIN ONE (after bulletin 1) only moves when you are not looking at it. Glance at it to freeze it. Stare for more than 3 seconds and it teleports behind you. Heavy static means it is close.

THE TALL ONES (after bulletin 7) are blind and hunt by sound. The MIC meter shows how loud you are: running is very loud, walking is loud, sneaking is quiet, standing still is silent. If one is coming, stop moving.

HOUSES have a lamp in the window. Go in through the door. Nothing can reach or hear you inside, and each house has one item on the floor.

CHAPTER 2 Houses are dark and no longer safe. Bunkers (red light over the door) are. RUNNERS chase your lamp: press L to switch it off, or use HUSH. ECHOES are invisible unless you use IR or SCAN, and tick faster as they get close. Starting a generator is heard by everything.

CAMERA Q cycles, or press 1-4.
1 SP: standard.  2 IR: sees far and lets you stare at the thin one safely, but the motor is loud.  3 HUSH: much quieter, but slower with the lamp off.  4 SCAN: shows both creatures in white, but hides the signal and the thin one speeds up.

ITEMS F flashbulb: drives off the thin one, very loud.  G decoy radio: thrown ahead, the tall ones go to it.  R tonic: refills stamina, no tiring for 10 s.

MOVEMENT SPACE jumps (silent in the air, loud landing). E dashes in the direction you are moving (loud, uses stamina). Press C while running to slide: fast and quiet. You move faster downhill and slower uphill. V toggles auto-run.

CONTROLS WASD move · mouse look (click to capture) · SHIFT run · C sneak · L lamp · H this guide · ESC or P pause. Touch: left side moves, right side looks, buttons on the right edge.
